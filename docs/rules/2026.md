# Dutch rules, 2026

This document records what the 2026 rule bundle covers, where every constant comes from, the order in which rounding is applied, the assumptions that remain, and what is deliberately excluded.

The current bundle is `nl-2026-b3`. It selects one immutable revision of each rule module:

| Rule ID                     | Domain                                                             |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `nl-employment-2026-r2`     | Holiday allowance, statutory leave, CAO Rijk IKB composition       |
| `nl-income-tax-2026-r2`     | Box 1 bands, general tax credit, labour tax credit                 |
| `nl-payroll-2026-r3`        | Periodic and special-remuneration withholding, contribution maxima |
| `nl-zorgtoeslag-2026-r2`    | Zorgtoeslag                                                        |
| `nl-huurtoeslag-2026-r2`    | Huurtoeslag                                                        |
| `nl-reimbursements-2026-r2` | Untaxed kilometre allowance                                        |

Rule modules are never edited in place. Each records its legal and effective period, publication date where available, verification date, official sources, and a canonical content hash over its identity, sources, and parameters. `src/rules/provenance.test.ts` pins every hash, so changing a constant fails the build until a new rule ID is minted. A calculation records the exact rule IDs and hashes it used.

Only `nl-2026-b3` and its selected rule revisions are supported. Superseded manifests and payroll rules are not exported or resolved.

## Supported scope

An ordinary Netherlands employee case for calendar year 2026:

- a domestic taxpayer below the AOW age;
- Box 1 employment income, explicitly supplied pre-tax employee pension deductions and taxable non-cash benefits; `otherAnnualIncome` is fixed at €0;
- zorgtoeslag with and without a benefit partner, for a client living in the Netherlands;
- ordinary huurtoeslag households;
- first-calendar-year and steady-state annual views.

## Constants

### Income tax

Box 1, below AOW age: up to and including € 38.883 at 35,75%; above € 38.883 up to and including € 78.426 at 37,56%; above € 78.426 at 49,50%. The first band is composed of AOW 17,90%, Anw 0,10%, Wlz 9,65%, and 8,10% wage tax.

General tax credit: € 3.115 up to and including € 29.736, then reduced by 6,398% of the excess, reaching zero at € 78.426.

Labour tax credit: 8,324% up to € 11.965; € 996 + 31,009% up to € 25.845; € 5.300 + 1,950% up to € 45.592; € 5.685 − 6,510% up to € 132.920; zero above.

Employee pension premiums withheld by the employer have already reduced the fiscal wage, so the engine never deducts them a second time.

### Payroll

The witte tabel bijzondere beloningen is reproduced exactly for employees below the AOW age living in the Netherlands. With the payroll tax credit applied the rate is the standaardtarief plus the verrekeningspercentage loonheffingskorting, which is negative while a credit is still building up. The leaflet instructs rounding the result to cents in the employee's favour.

The binding white monthly and four-week wage tables are generated from the official calculation rules: annualize the period wage, select the nearest lower €54 annual wage step through €133.110, apply the table's whole-euro tax and credit rounding, then convert back to the period. Above the published maximum period wage, 49,50% of the excess is rounded down to cents and added to the last table amount. All 2.465 rows in each PDF were checked in both below-AOW columns (with and without payroll tax credit): 9.860 comparisons, with no mismatch.

Loontijdvak maximum contribution wage: € 79.409 a year. Zvw employer levy 6,10%; the 4,85% employee withholding applies to categories outside the supported case and is recorded for completeness only.

### Zorgtoeslag

Standaardpremie € 2.119, or € 4.238 with a toeslagpartner. Drempelinkomen € 29.736. Normpremie is 1,912% (4,289% with a partner) of the drempelinkomen plus 13,73% of the excess of the joint toetsingsinkomen, where a negative second term counts as € 0. Income ceilings € 40.857 and € 51.142. Asset ceilings € 146.011 and € 184.633. Where the toeslagpartner is not insured under the Wet op de zorgtoeslag the allowance is halved. The client's own insurance premium is not an input.

### Huurtoeslag

Huurgrens € 498,20 below 21 without a child or handicap, otherwise € 932,93. Basishuur € 202,52 for one person and € 200,71 for two or more; from 2026 it no longer depends on income. Kwaliteitskortingsgrens € 498,20. Aftoppingsgrens € 713,02 for one or two persons and € 764,14 for three or more. Parts A, B, and C reimburse 100%, 65%, and 40% of successive rent slices. Inkomensijkpunt € 23.425 (one person, 27% taper) or € 31.500 (two or more, 22% taper), divided by twelve. Child income exemption € 6.218 for resident children under 23 at the start of the year. Asset ceilings € 38.479 single, € 76.958 with a partner, plus € 38.479 per co-resident. Service costs no longer count toward the rekenhuur.

### Employment and reimbursements

Holiday allowance: at least 8% of the gross annual salary, paid at least once a year. Statutory paid leave: four times the weekly working hours per year. CAO Rijk IKB budget: 16,50% of salary, minimum € 452 a month, with the 8% vakantie-uitkering contained inside it.

Untaxed kilometre allowance: € 0,25 per kilometre, raised from € 0,23 with retroactive effect to 1 January 2026.

## Rounding order

Money is integer euro cents and percentages are integer parts per million. Every intermediate value is an exact `bigint` fraction; rounding happens only at the points listed here.

**Income tax.** Taxable income and employment income are truncated to whole euros. Band tax and each credit are computed exactly and then rounded to cents, ties away from zero. The net tax is their difference, floored at zero.

**Special remuneration.** The payment is multiplied by the table rate and the withholding is rounded **down** to cents, as the leaflet instructs.

**Regular-period withholding.** The actual period wage selects the nearest lower published tabelloon. Annual table tax and credits use the official whole-euro rounding order, and the period result is rounded arithmetically to cents. Above the maximum table row, tax on the excess is rounded **down** to cents in the employee's favour.

**Zorgtoeslag.** The normpremie is **truncated** to cents. The third published example yields € 1.275,37704 and is printed as € 1.275,37, which rules out rounding up. The allowance is the standaardpremie minus the normpremie, floored at zero, halved where the partner is uninsured. The paid monthly amount is the annual amount divided by twelve, rounded **down** to whole euros.

**Huurtoeslag.** Parts A, B, and C and the income correction are carried as exact fractions and the monthly total is rounded **down** to whole euros once, at the end. The annual amount is twelve times the monthly amount.

A household in which every member is under 21 follows the leaflet's jongerenhuishouden route literally: step 4 is skipped, so there is no aftoppingsgrens and no parts B or C, and step 7 states that part A alone _is_ the monthly allowance, so the income-dependent correction (part D) is not applied either. A household is only treated this way when **every** member's age is known and below 21; when one member is 21 or older the ordinary route applies, and when an age is unknown no amount is produced.

## Assumptions

- The general and labour tax credits are settled in full for a complete calendar year of Dutch employment.
- The adapter derives a recurring period wage by evenly spreading recurring taxable wage and pre-tax deductions over 12 monthly or 13 four-week periods. Separately paid holiday allowance and year-end cash use the special-remuneration table. If actual taxable wage or deductions vary by period, a period-level contract is needed for payslip parity.
- Normal-period net salary subtracts recurring pre-tax pension, regular-table withholding, additional withholding, and explicitly entered post-tax deductions from normalized base pay. The estimated bank deposit then adds only the explicitly entered recurring net reimbursement. Annual net salary excludes reimbursements; the after-settlement view replaces payroll withholding with estimated final annual tax.
- Huurtoeslag assumes the household composition, rent, and income are unchanged for the whole allowance year.
- The steady-state view assumes a complete calendar year in the new job; the first-calendar-year view combines pre-start income with the new job's cash actually paid inside that year.
- Tax already withheld is carried as a settlement input. A refund is never counted as employer compensation, and neither allowance is compensation.

## Known source discrepancies

The current zorgtoeslag leaflet (TG 082 - 1Z62FD) corrects example 2 to € 103,62 before whole-euro rounding. Its annual allowance of € 1.243,51 and paid monthly amount of € 103 match the engine.

**Huurtoeslag rounds its parts inconsistently.** In example 2, part C of € 67,516 is printed as € 67,51, which is truncation. In example 3, the correction of € 125,4375 is printed as € 125,44, which is rounding up. The engine keeps every part exact and rounds once at the end, which reproduces all three published monthly results. The per-part cent amounts this engine reports may therefore differ from the leaflet by one cent without changing the paid amount.

## Periodic wage-table verification

The January 2026 monthly and four-week PDFs and version 2 of the official calculation rules are pinned by URL and SHA-256. The implementation was checked against every published row in the two below-AOW columns. The table operation is therefore exact for a supplied period wage inside the supported category. The application-level result can still be an estimate because the current annual offer contract derives an even recurring period wage rather than importing each real payslip's fiscal wage and deductions.

## Exclusions

Each of these returns a typed limitation and leaves the affected output unavailable. None falls through to an ordinary-case formula.

| Code | Excluded case |
| --- | --- |
| `nl_tax_aow_age_not_supported` | AOW age and older, and AOW-transition years |
| `nl_tax_non_domestic_not_supported` | Non-domestic taxpayers, international taxation, the expat scheme |
| `nl_payroll_special_remuneration_category_not_supported` | Special remuneration outside the supported employee category |
| `nl_payroll_regular_category_not_supported` | Periodic table categories outside a below-AOW employee living in the Netherlands |
| `nl_payroll_invalid_period_count` | Periodic withholding other than the monthly or four-week table |
| `nl_zorgtoeslag_non_resident_not_supported` | Zorgtoeslag with a woonlandfactor for residents abroad |
| `nl_huurtoeslag_exceptional_situation_not_supported` | Huurtoeslag cases needing a manual request |
| `nl_huurtoeslag_under21_with_child_or_handicap_unresolved` | An all-under-21 household whose rent limit is raised by a child or handicap; the published calculation does not resolve it |
| `nl_first_year_prior_income_required` | A first-calendar-year view without pre-start income |

Also out of scope for this release, and not implemented anywhere in this package: pension accrual value and unknown premium splits or bases, private company equity and option valuation, the 30% ruling, director-major-shareholder and self-employment rules, and municipal assistance.

## Updating for a new year or a corrected source

1. Re-open every source and its publication index to find newer editions; record the retrieval date, final URL, SHA-256, and changes. A working dated URL does not establish that its edition is current.
2. Add a **new** rule module with a new ID, for example `nl-income-tax-2027-r1`. Do not edit an existing module.
3. Add a new bundle ID selecting the new revisions.
4. Update the pinned hashes in `src/rules/provenance.test.ts` for the selected bundle and remove superseded definitions from exports and resolution.
5. Re-run the published examples as golden tests before publishing the bundle.
